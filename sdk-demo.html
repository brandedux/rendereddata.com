Bring your own layer

Drop in a GeoJSON, KML, GPX, PMTiles or GeoTIFF/COG file, or paste a file URL or an {z}/{x}/{y} tile template. Read in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Build your own world

    Paint an imaginary planet on the equirectangular template, then wrap it on the globe. Great for speculative worlds, games, anything. Reprojected in the browser; nothing is uploaded.

    Place search

      Draw & measure

      Pick a mode, click to add vertices. Double-click or Enter finishes; Esc cancels.

      Time animation (VIIRS)

      Steps the real NASA VIIRS daily mosaic across recent days.

      Wind particles

      Illustrative analytic field, not a live forecast. Pass field(lng,lat) to drive from real data.

      Celestial sky

      Real positions from src/celestial.js: J2000 catalogue stars coloured by spectral type, the Milky Way on the galactic plane, and the Sun, Moon and planets by ephemeris. The body is oriented by its IAU pole + prime meridian for the chosen time. Earth, the Moon and Mars appear as clickable hotspots in the sky: click one to fly there and swap the model.
