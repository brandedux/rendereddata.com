Bundled celestial-body textures — attribution
=============================================

These equirectangular surface maps are stored locally (never fetched at runtime) so the
scenes render offline and never depend on a third-party API.

earth.jpg    — NASA GIBS Blue Marble (Shaded Relief + Bathymetry), public domain
moon.jpg     — Solar System Scope (solarsystemscope.com/textures), CC BY 4.0
jupiter.jpg  — Solar System Scope (solarsystemscope.com/textures), CC BY 4.0
mars.jpg     — NASA / USGS Viking colour mosaic (via trek.nasa.gov), public domain

Solar System Scope textures are licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
free to use and modify with attribution to Solar System Scope. The maps themselves are based on
NASA elevation and imagery data.
