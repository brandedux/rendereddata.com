What you can do

From a bare globe to a living world.

Weather, geoscience, and planetary data on an open renderer. Every source you add makes the map more alive, and there is no edge to how far it goes.

From Earth to exoplanets

Earth, the Moon, and Mars today; new planets, moons, and exoplanets drop in as their data lands. One renderer draws them all, each in its own correct sky.

Alive with data

Live earthquake feeds, daily satellite imagery you can scrub by date, shaded 3D terrain, animated wind-and-current flow. The more sources you feed it, the more the map breathes. Built for data that changes by the hour.

Your data, your tools

Drop in GeoJSON, KML, or your own tiles, search places, and draw and measure straight on the globe. It all runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

As above, so below

One stack, from the lakebed to the stars.

Most maps stop at the coastline. Rendered Data Maps renders the whole vertical column: the basin beneath the water, the terrain that frames it, the air that moves across it, and the real sky it all turns under. One renderer, top to bottom.

  1. The sky

    Real stars, planets, and the Milky Way

    The actual celestial sphere from a catalogue of thousands of stars, placed for any moment in time and correctly oriented for Earth, the Moon, or Mars.

  2. The air

    Atmosphere and a live day-night line

    A glowing limb of air around the globe, and a terminator that tracks the real Sun as it sweeps the surface.

  3. The surface

    Imagery, borders, and live feeds

    Daily satellite imagery you can scrub by date, country lines, earthquakes as they happen, and your own data layered on top.

  4. The terrain

    Shaded relief and true 3D elevation

    Pitch the camera and the mountains rise. Hillshade and real DEM elevation give every ridge and valley its shape.

  5. Below the waterline

    Bathymetry and lakebeds

    The map does not stop at the shore. Relief and bathymetry carry the basin down to the bottom of the lake and the floor of the sea.

License-clear by design

Ship commercially without guessing.

Every source the map can touch arrives with its license, attribution, and commercial terms attached, and is probed live before it ever draws. If something is down, you see why; nothing fake is drawn in its place.
